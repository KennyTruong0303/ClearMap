How to review

  1. Start at R1. Scroll through the stack using the Z slider.
  2. Follow each candidate through adjacent planes. Click its center once, on its best-centered plane. Do not mark it again on neighboring planes.
  3. Use “Uncertain” when identity or separation is unclear. Use “Edge-truncated” for objects cut by any edge of this region, including the first/last Z plane.
  4. Mark the region reviewed only after inspecting every plane. Blank regions still need review.
  5. Download your labels before closing. This page does not save labels automatically.

Display: opaque grayscale, one fixed contrast range across Z within each region. Voxel spacing X/Y/Z: 2/2/5 µm. Colored circles show your marks on the current plane; dashed circles show adjacent-plane marks.
